# YouTube Video Research

## Workflow
1. Define research question
2. Search YouTube with targeted queries
3. Filter by relevance, date, channel authority
4. Extract key segments and claims
5. Cross-reference with other sources
6. Cite with timestamps

## Use Cases
- Expert interviews analysis
- Product demonstrations
- Conference talks and presentations
- Tutorial effectiveness evaluation
